How adding a teacher works

Click Teachers → + Add Teacher, enter their name, email and pick their department(s) (CSE, IT, ECE, CIVIL). The platform creates their account instantly with a temporary password and a Teacher ID (e.g. TCH1024). Share those credentials with the teacher however you like (email, WhatsApp, in person) — they can then log in from any computer, anywhere, and will be asked to set their own password on first login.
